For your EDA on the Pizza Database, you can start by exploring the data to understand its characteristics and relationships. Here are some key points and KPIs you can consider:

1. **Total Revenue:** Calculate the total revenue generated from pizza sales over a specific period.

2. **Average Order Value:** Determine the average amount spent per order.

3. **Popular Pizza Sizes:** Identify the most commonly ordered pizza sizes.

4. **Popular Pizza Categories:** Determine the most popular categories of pizzas (e.g., vegetarian, non-vegetarian).

5. **Sales Trends Over Time:** Analyze how pizza sales have trended over different time periods (daily, weekly, monthly).

6. **Peak Order Times:** Identify the times of day when pizza orders are highest.

7. **Customer Segmentation:** Segment customers based on their ordering behavior (e.g., frequent vs. occasional customers).

8. **Top Selling Pizzas:** Identify the best-selling pizza names or categories.

9. **Order Frequency:** Determine how often customers place orders.

10. **Customer Loyalty:** Analyze repeat orders from customers to measure loyalty.

11. **Seasonal Variations:** Identify any seasonal trends in pizza sales (e.g., more sales during holidays or weekends).

12. **Profit Margin:** Calculate the profit margin for each pizza sold.

13. **Discount Analysis:** Analyze the impact of discounts on sales and profit.

14. **Order Fulfillment Time:** Calculate the average time taken to fulfill an order.

15. **Customer Satisfaction:** If available, analyze customer feedback or ratings to gauge satisfaction levels.

These KPIs can provide valuable insights into your pizza sales data, helping you understand customer preferences, optimize pricing strategies, and improve overall business performance.

For your **Fashion Retail Sales** dataset, you can derive several **KPIs** and perform various analyses to build an insightful dashboard. Here’s a structured approach:  

---

### **Key Performance Indicators (KPIs):**  
1. **Total Revenue:** Sum of `Purchase Amount (USD)`.  
2. **Average Order Value (AOV):** `Total Revenue / Total Orders`.  
3. **Total Orders:** Count of `Customer Reference ID`.  
4. **Number of Unique Customers:** Count of distinct `Customer Reference ID`.  
5. **Customer Retention Rate:** % of repeat customers (`Customer Reference ID` appearing more than once).  
6. **Sales by Day of the Week:** Identify which day has the highest/lowest sales.  
7. **Sales by Month:** Track revenue trends over different months.  
8. **Payment Method Distribution:** Analyze customer payment preferences.  
9. **Average Review Rating:** `AVG(Review Rating)` to understand customer satisfaction.  
10. **Sales by Product Category:** If `Item Purchased` includes categories, determine best-selling categories.  

---

### **Analysis & Questions to Solve for the Dashboard:**  

#### **1. Sales Performance Analysis**
- What is the **total revenue** generated over time?  
- What is the **trend of sales** by month? Are there any seasonal peaks?  
- What is the **best-selling item** (most purchased item)?  

#### **2. Customer Behavior & Segmentation**
- How many **new vs. returning customers** are there?  
- What is the **average spend per customer**?  
- Who are the **top 10 highest spending customers**?  

#### **3. Payment & Purchase Patterns**
- What is the **most used payment method**?  
- How does **purchase behavior change by day of the week**? (Are weekends busier than weekdays?)  
- What is the **average review rating**? Which products have the highest/lowest ratings?  

#### **4. Time-Based Analysis**
- How does sales performance **vary by month**?  
- Which **day of the week generates the most sales**?  
- What are the **peak purchase hours**? (If time is available)  

---

### **Dashboard Structure & Insights:**  

📌 **Tab 1: Overview** (KPIs - Total Sales, AOV, Best Product, Repeat Customers)  
📌 **Tab 2: Sales Trends** (Revenue by Month, Day of Week, Payment Method Distribution)  
📌 **Tab 3: Customer Insights** (New vs. Returning, Top Customers, Review Ratings)
